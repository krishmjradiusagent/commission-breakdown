Commission Breakdown — flow map

Every role, status, sheet, popup and toast in the Commission Breakdown flow, laid out together. Each card is Commission breakdown.html embedded live and driven into that exact state — always matches the prototype, nothing redrawn.

Roles: Mark Perez (agent), Andy Martin (team lead), Jessica Hall (Radius auditing). Statuses: Awaiting agent confirmation → Awaiting team lead → Awaiting Radius auditing → Finalized, with Returned for edits as the loop-back.

1Role × status matrix

The same breakdown, seen by each role at each stage. Action bar, banner color and available actions all change with status; math shown in the hero changes with role.

2Edge cases

States that don't fit the happy path: missing wire instructions blocking finalization, a CDA deleted after finalizing, and a co-op side with no Radius agent.

3Popups & sheets

Every bottom-sheet in the flow, including the confirmation and return-for-edits popups the devs asked about.
