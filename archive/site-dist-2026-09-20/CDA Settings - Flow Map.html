CDA Settings — flow map

Every screen, role state, sheet and popup in the CDA Settings flow, laid out in order. Each card is the live prototype embedded and driven into that exact state — not a redrawn mock — so it always matches what's in CDA Settings.html and its sub-screens.

These screens confirm saves with an inline button morph (icon + label flip to a checkmark and "Saved"/"Assigned"), not a toast or modal. That's the actual success pattern here — see it live in the Add/Assign sheet cards below.
Settings→ CDA Settings→ Group Lead Plan Creation/ Commission Plan/ Fee Type/ Post Cap Plan/ Wiring Payment→ Pending Sub-Plan Approval

1CDA Settings — role gating

The row list is role-scoped: Team Lead sees everything including Group Lead Plan Creation; Group Lead loses that row; Agent only sees Wiring Payment plus a note that plans/fees are managed by the Team Lead.

2Group Lead Plan Creation

Team Lead toggles which Group Leads may create their own commission plans.

3Commission Plan

List of standard/tiered plans, per-plan Assign Defaults, and the Add/Edit sheet with live split validation.

4Fee Type

Reusable CDA deductions — flat or percentage, pre/post-split, optional cap contribution and sliding scale tiers.

5Post Cap Plan

Fees that kick in once an agent hits cap. Includes the archive confirmation popup.

6Wiring Payment

Team Wire, My Wire and Private Recipients. Includes an incomplete recipient, the add/edit sheet, and the delete confirmation popup.

7Pending Sub-Plan Approval

Team Lead approves or rejects Group-Lead-authored sub-plans. Rejection requires a reason; approving/rejecting the last item shows the empty state.
